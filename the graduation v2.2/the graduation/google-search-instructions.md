# Google Search Console Setup Instructions

## Overview
This document provides instructions for submitting and verifying your graduation website with Google Search Console to ensure it appears in search results when people search for the following terms:
- تخرج مدارس الانجاز 1446
- تخرج 1446
- graduation 1446
- alinjaz graduation 1446

## Step 1: Create a Google Search Console Account
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Start now"

## Step 2: Add Your Website Property
1. Select "URL prefix" property type
2. Enter your website URL: `https://alinjazschool.com/graduation1446/`
3. Click "Continue"

## Step 3: Verify Ownership
Choose one of these verification methods:
- **HTML file upload**: Download the HTML verification file from Google and upload it to your web server
- **HTML tag**: Add the meta tag to your site's `<head>` section
- **DNS record**: Add the TXT record to your domain's DNS configuration
- **Google Analytics**: If you already use Google Analytics, link your accounts

## Step 4: Submit Your Sitemap
1. In the left sidebar, click "Sitemaps"
2. Enter `sitemap.xml` in the field
3. Click "Submit"

## Step 5: Monitor Performance for Keywords
Once verified, use the "Performance" section to track how your site ranks for these keywords:
- تخرج مدارس الانجاز 1446
- تخرج 1446
- graduation 1446
- alinjaz graduation 1446

## Step 6: Optimize Performance (If Needed)
If your site isn't ranking well for these terms:
1. Check the "Coverage" section for any indexing issues
2. Review "Mobile Usability" to ensure your site works well on phones
3. Use the "URL Inspection" tool to check specific pages

## Step 7: Track Search Analytics
Monitor these metrics for your target keywords:
- Total clicks
- Total impressions 
- Average click-through rate (CTR)
- Average position in search results

## Additional Tips
- Make sure all images have descriptive alt text in Arabic and English
- Consider adding more content that naturally includes these keywords
- Link to your site from social media using these keywords in the post text
- Check "Search appearance" → "Search results" to see how your site appears in Google search

## Testing Your Site's Visibility
After setup is complete, search for these terms on Google to see your site's position:
- `تخرج مدارس الانجاز 1446`
- `تخرج 1446`
- `graduation 1446`
- `alinjaz graduation 1446`

If the site doesn't appear within 2-4 weeks, revisit the optimization steps above.
