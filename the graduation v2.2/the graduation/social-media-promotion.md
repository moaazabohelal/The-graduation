# Social Media Promotion Strategy

## Target Search Terms
This guide will help you promote your website on social media to increase visibility when people search for:
- تخرج مدارس الانجاز 1446
- تخرج 1446
- graduation 1446
- alinjaz graduation 1446

## Platform-Specific Strategies

### Instagram
1. **Post Frequency**: 3-4 posts per week
2. **Content Ideas**:
   - Share graduation photos with hashtag #تخرج_مدارس_الانجاز_1446
   - Post short reels showcasing student projects
   - Create countdown stories to the graduation event
   - Share behind-the-scenes of project development
3. **Hashtags to Use**:
   - #تخرج_مدارس_الانجاز_1446
   - #تخرج_1446
   - #alinjaz_graduation_1446
   - #graduation_1446
   - #مدارس_الانجاز

### Twitter/X
1. **Post Frequency**: Daily tweets
2. **Content Ideas**:
   - Share project highlights with the website link
   - Create polls about favorite projects
   - Live tweet during graduation events
   - Share student success stories
3. **Hashtags to Use**:
   - Same as Instagram, plus trending educational hashtags

### Facebook
1. **Post Frequency**: 2-3 posts per week
2. **Content Ideas**:
   - Create a dedicated event page for "تخرج مدارس الانجاز 1446"
   - Share photo albums of student projects
   - Post longer-form content about the graduation
   - Create a community discussion group
3. **Tips**:
   - Always include the website URL in posts
   - Tag relevant educational pages
   - Use both Arabic and English in posts

### Snapchat
1. **Post Frequency**: Daily stories during peak periods
2. **Content Ideas**:
   - Create graduation filters with "تخرج 1446" text
   - Share quick snapshots of project development
   - Create location-based stories during events
   - Share QR codes linking to the website

## Content Calendar

### Pre-Graduation (1 Month Before)
- Week 1: Introduce the graduation theme and website
- Week 2: Highlight student preparation
- Week 3: Showcase previews of projects
- Week 4: Build excitement with countdown posts

### During Graduation Week
- Day 1-3: Share live updates from events
- Day 4-5: Post student interviews
- Day 6-7: Highlight project presentations

### Post-Graduation (2 Weeks After)
- Week 1: Share graduation ceremony highlights
- Week 2: Post "best of" project compilations

## Engagement Strategies
1. **Respond to Comments**: Reply to all comments within 24 hours
2. **Cross-Promotion**: Tag students and encourage them to share
3. **Influencer Outreach**: Collaborate with local education influencers
4. **Paid Promotion**: Consider targeted ads using the search keywords

## Tracking Results
1. Use UTM parameters to track social media traffic:
   - `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=graduation1446`
2. Monitor which platforms drive the most traffic
3. Check which search terms people use to find your content

## Best Practices
1. Always include a clear call-to-action
2. Use high-quality images and videos
3. Mix Arabic and English content
4. Pin important posts with website links
5. Add website links to all social media profiles
6. Create shareable content that includes your target search terms
